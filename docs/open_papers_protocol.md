# Paper packing on open benchmarks (P-2), frozen before any code or run

P-1 ([`papers_protocol.md`](papers_protocol.md)) found three causes of missed evidence on 20
forecasting papers: the 12 000-token budget fits one paper (14 of 24 missed cross-paper quotes),
one window per paper misses quotes elsewhere in it (7 single-paper misses, all 8 W losses), and
low ranking of the second paper (10 misses). Those causes were read off P-1's own 50 questions, so
P-2 measures the fixes on two open benchmarks with a dev split for choosing settings and a test
split scored once.

## Benchmarks (fixed before any run)

| | QASPER ([Dasigi et al., NAACL 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03011)) | MDA-QA ([Huang et al., EMNLP Findings 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.576/)) |
|---|---|---|
| License | CC BY 4.0 | CC BY 4.0 (paper text: SPIQA extracted paragraphs, CC BY 4.0) |
| Questions | written by NLP researchers about one paper | generated by GPT-4o, filtered, need 2+ papers |
| Gold | evidence paragraphs per annotator | arXiv ids of the support papers |
| Dev | official dev split: 281 papers | MDA-QA ids shuffled with `random.Random(0)`: the first 200 |
| Test | official test split: 416 papers | the next 100 |
| Corpus of a split | the split's papers | the union of the split's support papers |

- QASPER v0.3 (`qasper-dev-v0.3.json`, `qasper-test-v0.3.json`). Questions: those with at least one
  annotator answer that is answerable and has at least one evidence paragraph not starting with
  `FLOAT SELECTED` (dev 892, test 1 310). Paper text: for each section, its name and its paragraphs,
  joined by blank lines. QASPER questions refer to their paper as "they", so the query is
  `In the paper "<title>": <question>` (like P-1's named questions).
- MDA-QA (`MDA-QA.json`, 6 804 questions, 2.06 support papers on average). Paper text: the SPIQA
  file `<arxiv id>v<n>.txt` (highest `n`). Query: the question as given.
- The raw files stay in `data/raw/open_papers/` (gitignored); the splits are reproducible from the
  rules above.

## Systems

All use the shipped `hybrid+ce@100` over recursive 500 / 50 chunks (BM25 and bge-small top 100,
RRF k = 60, MiniLM reranks the top 100), a pool of the top 10 chunks, overflow `stop`. A packing
configuration is (budget, window, slices):

- budget: 12 000 (shipped), 24 000 or 36 000 estimated tokens;
- window: none (shipped: whole document, a first document over the budget is cut to the
  budget-sized window and packing ends), 4 000 or 8 000 tokens (P-1's W: a longer document is cut to
  the window and packing continues);
- slices: 1 (shipped: one window centred on the document's best chunk) or 3: a document being cut to
  width w is represented by up to 3 windows of width w / 3 centred on its first 3 chunks in the pool
  (fewer if fewer are in the pool), overlapping windows merged, kept in document order and joined by
  a blank line.

B0 = (12 000, none, 1). On the dev splits all 18 configurations are run. For each budget b, S_b is
the (window, slices) pair with the highest mean of the two dev primary metrics; ties within 0.005
go to window none, then slices 1. The test splits are scored once for B0, S_12000, S_24000 and
S_36000.

**Contexts (WC).** MDA-QA test only (16 400 estimated chunks; QASPER queries name the paper, so
contexts cannot help find it). P-1's recipe: Anthropic's prompt, `qwen3:8b`, temperature 0, at most
200 output tokens, thinking off, `num_ctx` 32 768, document cut to its first 60 000 characters,
prepended to the chunk for BM25, the embedder and the cross-encoder; the windows are located with
the plain chunk text. Before generation, a probe (P-1 amendment 4) sends every paper's cut text
with its longest chunk; if any prompt exceeds 32 568 tokens, the cut becomes 50 000 characters for
all papers. Contexts run on a private Ollama limited to one free GPU (P-1 amendment 3), never on the
shared server. WC_b = S_b with contexts. Committed: `docs/sota-runs/open_papers_contexts.json`.

## Metrics

- QASPER, **evidence recall** (primary): per question, the maximum over qualifying annotators of the
  share of their text evidence paragraphs contained in the packed context (lowercase, whitespace
  collapsed).
- MDA-QA, **support recall** (primary): per question, the share of support papers with any packed
  text. MDA-QA has no evidence spans, so a packed window of the right paper counts even if it misses
  the part the answer needs.
- Reported, not gating: all support papers packed (MDA-QA), MRR of the first gold paper in the
  chunk ranking, packed tokens, distinct papers packed.

## Decision rule

Paired bootstrap over questions (10 000 resamples, seed 0, 95 % CI).

1. **Packing at the shipped budget.** If S_12000 differs from B0, it becomes the `search()` default
   iff on test S_12000 - B0 has CI lower bound > 0 on MDA-QA, CI lower bound > -0.02 on QASPER, and
   the EU guard holds (packed output identical to B0 on the 190 pooled EU questions).
2. **Budget.** S_24000 - B0 and S_36000 - B0 are reported with their token cost; the default budget
   is not changed by this study (it changes EU output and the agent's cost, so it is the user's
   call).
3. **Contexts.** Recommended for paper corpora (generated at upload) iff WC_12000 - S_12000 has CI
   lower bound > 0 on MDA-QA test support recall. WC_24000 and WC_36000 are reported.

## Predictions (written before any run)

- P1: on MDA-QA, B0 support recall is 0.40 to 0.60 (a SPIQA paper is about 8 700 estimated tokens,
  so one of two support papers fits); S_36000 - B0 is +0.20 to +0.35.
- P2: on QASPER, B0 evidence recall is at least 0.80 (median paper about 5 200 tokens, title in the
  query); every S_b - B0 is within ±0.03.
- P3: S_12000 uses a window (4 000 or 8 000), because MDA-QA needs a second paper.
- P4: WC_12000 - S_12000 is +0.02 to +0.08 on MDA-QA.
- P-cost: dev grid and test scoring under 1 hour on the laptop; contexts about 11 hours on one shared
  GPU.

## Not claimed

Answer quality through the agent; QASPER questions without the paper title ("described" questions);
evidence inside MDA-QA support papers; the group's own papers.

## Amendment 1 (a dev run and a probe were started and stopped; no output exists)

1. **Equal MDA-QA splits.** Each MDA-QA corpus is the union of its own questions' support papers,
   so its size sets how crowded the ranking is: dev 200 questions (410 papers) against test 100
   (206) would tune on a pool twice as crowded as the one scored. Dev becomes the first 100
   shuffled ids (201 papers), test the next 100 (209 papers). No unrelated distractor papers are
   added: they would double the context calls, and every corpus paper already is a distractor for
   the other questions.
2. **QASPER's role.** Measured paper lengths (estimated tokens): of 416 test papers, 282 exceed
   4 000, 52 exceed 8 000 and 8 exceed 12 000. B0 almost always packs the whole gold paper, so
   QASPER cannot show a budget gain; it is the guard against windows and slices losing evidence
   (P-1's described-question loss), which is how rule 1 uses it (non-inferiority, -0.02).
3. **Token units.** Budgets are ragkit's estimated tokens (characters / 4). P-1 measured paper text
   at 2.1 to 3.1 characters per Qwen token, so 12 000 / 24 000 / 36 000 estimated tokens are
   about 15 000 to 23 000 / 31 000 to 46 000 / 46 000 to 69 000 real tokens; costs are reported
   with that range.

## Outcome

Dev grid [`sota-runs/open_papers_dev.txt`](sota-runs/open_papers_dev.txt) chose S_12000 =
12000/4000/1, S_24000 = 24000/8000/1, S_36000 = 36000/none/1. Test scored once:
[`sota-runs/open_papers_score.txt`](sota-runs/open_papers_score.txt). Contexts: 19 272 chunks (not
the estimated 16 400), mean 30.9 words, 0 empty, no prompt cut (probe: at most 23 666 tokens).

| Test | QASPER evidence recall (1 310) | MDA-QA support recall (100) | QASPER tokens | MDA-QA tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B0 | 0.921 | 0.468 | 7 881 | 8 800 |
| S_12000 - B0 | -0.125 [-0.148, -0.104] | +0.255 [+0.203, +0.308] | 8 734 | 9 583 |
| S_24000 - B0 | +0.043 [+0.031, +0.056] | +0.255 [+0.203, +0.308] | 14 732 | 18 027 |
| S_36000 - B0 | +0.061 [+0.048, +0.074] | +0.267 [+0.213, +0.322] | 18 555 | 22 265 |
| WC_12000 - S_12000 | | -0.007 [-0.045, +0.032] | | 8 653 |

Tokens are mean estimated tokens per search (characters / 4); real tokens are about 1.3 to 1.9
times more (amendment 1).

**Verdicts.** Rule 1: S_12000 not adopted (QASPER lower bound -0.148 < -0.02; EU guard held).
Rule 3: contexts not recommended. Rule 2: S_36000 gains on both benchmarks (81 better, 0 worse on
QASPER) at about 2.4 times the tokens of B0; the default budget stays the user's call.

- P1 held: B0 support recall 0.468, S_36000 - B0 +0.267.
- P2 failed: QASPER B0 is 0.921, but S_12000 loses 0.125 and the larger budgets gain 0.043 and
  0.061. The budget matters on QASPER too: B0 packs the gold paper for 0.924 of the questions,
  S_36000 for 0.982 (gold paper MRR 0.930 for all systems).
- P3 held: S_12000 uses the 4 000-token window.
- P4 failed: contexts raise paper MRR (0.971 vs 0.939) but not support recall (-0.007).
- P-cost: dev grid and test scoring took about 12 minutes; contexts about 14 hours (shared GPU,
  six tunnel drops resumed from the cache).
- Measured after scoring, not gating (throwaway script, B0 ranking on MDA-QA test): the share of
  support papers with a chunk in the top 10 / 20 / 50 / 100 chunks is 0.773 / 0.875 / 0.950 /
  0.977. S_36000 (0.735) is close to the top-10 ceiling, so on multi-paper questions the pool of 10
  chunks, not the budget, now limits recall. Three slices per window were chosen at no budget.
